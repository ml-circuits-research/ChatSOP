- **`reasoning/slice/`.** `demand.mjs` (a static magic-set style plan: conjunctions ordered by sideways information passing, head positions seeded only when every call site binds them, scans only for atoms no constant reaches, run-time value domains), `retrieval.mjs` (`SliceRetrieval`: keyed lookups of both polarities through the registered memory strategy, fixpoint of the demand, incremental widening of the cap and the lookup budget, fact/probe/lookup/time budgets, a small relation read whole when a join would look it up value by value), `source.mjs`, `guard.mjs` (`judge`), `answer.mjs` (`answerOverSlice`: ask, judge, widen, report), `wire.mjs` (`Theory`, `askMemory`, `judgeWire`).
- **Chat path.** `reasoning/linker.mjs` `linkKnowledge` keeps its signature and result and retrieves through `SliceRetrieval` (the goal planner still picks the rules); the `reason` step of `sop/runtime.mjs` runs `answerOverSlice` for deduce, temporal and classify. A count, a universal answer no counterexample decided, a ranking and a failure-derived unknown over a slice that is not settled (complete and exact) are `status: incomplete`, `reason: partial_retrieval` (a count keeps `at_least`); proofs, counterexamples and opposing facts stay valid and say `complete: false`; every packet has `retrieval` (R-P5). New: Runtime option `closed(p)` and policy `closedWorld` (`view`, default; `declared`: R-P4); `sop/cnl.mjs` renders `incomplete` and `at_least`.
- **Knowledge-wire path.** `POST /v1/sessions/{id}/query` answers through `askMemory`: the rules, defaults, aggregates and predicate declarations that can reach the query plus the facts they need go to the oracle; the decision reads the oracle's `sensitivity`, so `absent`, aggregates and default conclusions are judged on the desugared program. Before, the whole theory text went to the engine.
- **Latency of a large SQLite memory.** `memory/banks/sqlite.mjs` rebuilds a bank lazily (domains read from the stored rows); `memory/repository.mjs` caches the walked snapshot chain by its heads and shares decoded snapshots across the repositories of the process (`CHATSOP_SHARED_SNAPSHOTS`); `memory/temporal.mjs` recalls through a per-layer index by tuple hash (frozen snapshot layers indexed once). world-v1 (349,364 facts, 219 snapshots): reading a session's memory 9.7 s on every lookup before, 3.9 s once per process and 0 ms after; the three acceptance questions of `tools/linking/acceptance-chat.mjs` through the HTTP chat took 162.7, 92.3 and 89.3 s, and take 16.5 s for the first question of a fresh server (12.3 s of it the SymbolicLM start, the same on the empty `default` base) and 0.4 to 0.9 s after (3.2 s for the first question that touches new predicates).
- **Tests and tools.** `tests/slice-retrieval.test.mjs` (38 tests: smoke cases 20 to 24 on real SQLite repositories, R-P1 to R-P5, the demand plan, the guard, the knowledge-wire path); `tests/outputs.test.mjs` asks the count of a SQLite memory and asserts that an associative memory withholds it; `tools/eval/slice-timing.mjs` writes `eval/reports/current/slice-path/timing.json`. DS005, DS006, DS018, DS022 and `docs/runtime.html` describe the path.
