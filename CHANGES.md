# Composed evaluation, content-word overlap, form variants and the production regression loop (evaluation is an engineering check)

On 2026-09-30 the owner decided that evaluation is an engineering check: a test may repeat a grammatical form of the training side but must not duplicate a case, and the only generalization tested is the same form with different words. `node tools/datasets/audit/content-word-overlap.mjs` compares every sealed test row with train and dev (exact and normalized text duplicates; the content-word signature of names, nouns and verbs; the form signature of the forms inventory) and fails closed, through `tools/datasets/verify-three-datasets.mjs`, on a text duplicate and on a lexical duplicate (identical content words and form); `node check-datasets.mjs` prints the numbers. `node tools/datasets/drop-lexical-duplicates.mjs --apply` removes the train/dev rows that duplicate a sealed row. Form variants fill the form gaps in both directions by slot substitution, verified by SymbolicLM: `tools/datasets/form-variants.mjs` (working data for test forms the training side lacks, from a delexicalized template catalog that the auditor-side `tools/eval/form-templates.mjs` writes, so no generator reads a sealed row) and `tools/eval/test-variants.mjs` (sealed `eval/suites/symbolic_english/test-variants.jsonl` for train forms the test lacks). `tools/datasets/composed-train.mjs` adds long composed working-data cases (paragraphs of 2 to 8 sentences: identity, mixed with neuro sentences replaced by their targets, and decomposition) built from train and dev rows only.

The composed evaluation suites (`node tools/eval/composed-suites.mjs`, deterministic, sealed test rows only) are `eval/suites/<dataset>/test-composed.jsonl` with `manifest-composed.json`, shown in the audit page as the split `test-composed`: K1 all symbolic (2, 3, 5, 8 sentences), K2 mixed symbolic and neuro, K3 identity long (up to 48 sentences, token lengths in a sidecar beyond the proofreader's training maximum of 547), K4 bad_english mixed, K5 reference across sentences, K6 decomposition. `node tools/eval/composed-score.mjs` scores SymbolicLM alone and any rewriter (endpoint or command), whole paragraph or per sentence; the preregistration is `status/preregistrations/eval-composed-v1.json`. Decomposition (short simple sentences with explicit subjects) is the main job of both proofing models, and the target-style contract is DS021 "Limited English for SymbolicLM" with its coverage analysis (`tools/datasets/audit/decomposition-coverage.mjs`). `tools/datasets/add-case.mjs` (add, review, merge production cases through `datasets/<dataset>/incoming.jsonl`) and `tools/datasets/harvest.mjs` (bad_english through the chain, the chain's failures into neuro_english) implement the loop of DS008 "How we learn from production".

# TranslatorService and LanguagesUtil split out of SymbolicLM; Romanian's default route becomes `direct` (Q-PIPE-1, data-driven)

On 2026-09-30 the owner's decision to separate concerns inside the symbolic path was implemented. Per-token language identification, matrix-language (frame) detection and the symbolic spelling corrector moved the same way (also verified byte-identical, with and without `--spell`) into a new **LanguagesUtil** (`lib/languages-util/`, DS021 "LanguagesUtil": `langid.mjs`, the new `frame.mjs` `detectFrame`, `spellfix.mjs`). SymbolicLM (`lib/symbolic-lm/`) now owns only UD parsing, SOP generation, the uncertainty signal and routing, calling both new components.

A preregistered comparison (`eval-translator-compare-v1`, `status/preregistrations/eval-translator-compare-v1.json`, `eval/reports/current/translator-compare/summary.md`) then answered Q-PIPE-1 (questions.md) by numbers rather than opinion, on a fresh 150-row monolingual-Romanian / 100-row mixed slice of `formalizer-v1` train.jsonl (dev.jsonl is fully consumed by `eval-symbolic-lm-v1`) plus a 40-message niche-word probe. Apertium was dropped before running: no Romanian→English pair exists, packaged or in the `apertium` GitHub organization's 655 repositories. On Romanian, no arm beat `direct` (symbolic-hybrid +4.7 [-1.3,+10.7]) and Opus-MT was decisively worse (-15.3 [-22.7,-8.0], also ~6× slower) — the second independent experiment, after `eval-symbolic-lm-v1`, to find this, so **Romanian's default route changes from `translate` to `direct`** (`DEFAULT_ROUTES` in `lib/symbolic-lm/index.mjs`; both superseded questions Q-SLM-1 and Q-PIPE-1 removed from `questions.md`). On mixed messages, `symbolic`/`symbolic-hybrid` led `direct` by +7.0 points with a 95% CI of exactly [0.0, +14.0] — the closest possible miss of the decisive threshold — so mixed messages keep their current default (`translate`, the `symbolic` backend); a new `matrix` route (frame-language detection from closed-class function words, `lib/languages-util/frame.mjs`) was not decisively different from direct and is not adopted. Owner question 1 (can Opus-MT clean up "romgleza"?): no, not reliably — only 21/50 (42%) of a manually read mixed-message sample were fully fluent English, the rest keeping at least one untranslated Romanian function word or clause. Owner question 2 (unknown words): the symbolic backend is far safer on a 40-message niche-word probe (70% correct, 21% honestly copied and reported `untranslated`, 4% hallucinated) than Opus-MT (32% correct, 21% hallucinated, a generative model with no "I don't know" mechanism); two symbolic-backend hallucinations trace to real dictionary-entry bugs (`girant`→"manager", `user`→"royal curt usher"), left open as a follow-up. A pipeline-level finding: `lib/ud-to-sop/protect.mjs`'s name-masking heuristic checks a sentence-initial capitalized word only against an English common-word list, so it mismasks an ordinary Romanian common noun at a Romanian sentence's start. No training, no GPU.

# SOP Lang: one clause per wire, clause links, unparsed spans and a host dictionary

On 2026-09-29 the one current model language gained what the owner decided (D1 and its refinement, L1–L4) and one more wire type. The small model no longer translates: content words stay in the message's language, normalized (relation lemmas such as `"lucra la"`, common nouns in the nominative), and a deterministic host step translates them with a reviewed bilingual and synonym dictionary (`sop/dictionary.mjs`, `config/dictionary/` from reviewed hand entries, the generator's constructions and filtered English Wiktionary under CC BY-SA 4.0; `tools/dictionary.mjs`); an unknown word is reported `untranslated`, never guessed. One finite clause is one wire, related by the conjunction keywords `because`, `so`, `if`, `unless`, `although`, `so_that`, `before`, `after`, `when`, `while` with `$id` (`sop/enums.mjs` `LINK_KEYWORDS`); a role value may be `$id` (a clause as an argument, or `$q`, the answers of an earlier query, joined by the host). The host scopes an `if`/`unless` supposition to its query, bounds a query by a timed `before`/`after`/`when`/`while` clause, and reports the other links as `not_checked` (`sop/clauses.mjs`). The new `unparsed` wire marks a verbatim span the model could not formalize; the host repairs it symbolically (dates, numbers, money, names, the dictionary, the conversation; `sop/repair.mjs`) or asks one question per span, and answers the rest. The packet reports `clause_links`, `translations`, `untranslated`, `repairs` and `unresolved_spans`. The evaluation scores strict (no dictionary) and tolerant (dictionary and synonyms) execution and adds honesty and understood-part metrics (DS016); `tools/datasets/convert-targets.mjs` converts old targets where the mapping is mechanical. Specifications: DS021, DS004, DS022, DS016, DS014; wire help pages including `unparsed.html`. No training.

# Context-free small-model surface sop-agent-4

The small formalizer now sees only the user's message and writes `stated`, `assumed`, `unclear`, `query` (string `match` blocks) and `constraint` as quoted strings with a closed role inventory; the host links them to the reviewed lexicon and asks when a link is unknown or ambiguous. Asserted statements are turn-local evidence carried in the conversation, suppositions and reported speech are conditional, model assumptions are reported (or branched by host policy), `unclear` has two kinds with fixed EN/RO replies, and an understood question without an engine is answered as not computable. `premise` is retired from the model surface and kept for trusted circuits and legacy `sop-agent-3` evaluation. The `allowJsEval` policy is removed, `cnl.language` defaults to English, and the chat API and page accept an answer language. See DS021.

# Declarative model boundary and whitespace atom cutover

SOP atoms now use whitespace-separated terms, for example `temperature room_a 21`; the old parenthesized spelling is rejected. The session-recording wire is `remember`, with no `assert` alias. JavaScript assertions, solver languages, source quotations and explicitly archived historical artifacts retain their own syntax.

The small model may author only `premise`, `query` and `constraint`. Runtime admission and prompts enforce that boundary. Host-approved orchestration produces separately inspectable resolution, assumption packs, solves, checked scalar projections and CNL. Blocking identity or scalar ambiguity produces a host-authored clarification with the pending declarative problem for the next turn; an entailed claim does not justify selecting an ambiguous requested value. Missing query evidence remains unknown.

Premises carry host-attributed model-interpretation provenance and remain conditional conversation context across turns, never implicit repository facts. Conditionality survives query-to-scalar-to-numeric chains. Independently admitted sourced facts and explicit trusted `remember` operations remain separate.
