# Coordinated questions follow grammar only (rules v2.8), one result packet for the operations, one name for a wall-clock cut (2026-10-01, decisions-agent)

Owner principle of 2026-10-01: as correct and as generic as possible, no hacks, nothing that cannot go to production; technical choices are decided, not asked (journal "Technical choices are decided by principle, not asked"). Q-QUERY-1 and Q-REASON-1 are decided and implemented. No training, no GPU, no port 9999, no commit.

- **A. Rules v2.8 (`lib/ud-to-sop/labels.mjs`).** The v2.7 same-subject heuristic is removed: `readLabels` no longer joins two auxiliary-initial yes/no clauses ("Does Ana go to X and does she attend Y?") into one conjunction, and `applyLabels` no longer inspects subjects. Only an explicit "both" ("Both at once: A, and B?", "Does Ana both work at X and live in Y?") merges the question clauses of a sentence into one `where all` query, and never when a clause has a period of its own (`during`, `at`, `overlaps`); predicates conjoined inside one clause stay one conjunctive query by their grammar. Every other question clause is its own query. "still", "again" and "anymore" keep the `assumed ... basis implicature` presupposition, and `overlaps` stays. `RULES_VERSION` is `ud-rules-v2.8` (`tools/datasets/three-datasets/score.mjs`); the snapshot is frozen in `eval/reports/history/baseline-ud-rules/frozen-rules-v2.8`. DS021 "Query forms of the rules".
- **A. Regression (`symbolic_english`, 8,052 rows).** v2.8 differs from v2.7 on 37 rows: the grammar-only reading brings 22 gold-verified rows that v2.7 broke (the gold has one query per clause) back to their gold, 10 no-gold rows and 4 equivalent rows back to the stored baseline, and 1 row changes in another way. The whole regression (v2.7 and v2.8 against the stored baseline, which predates v2.7) was re-baselined with `node tools/symbolic-regression.mjs --update`; every changed row is explained by cause in `eval/reports/current/query-rules/regression-diff-v2.8.md`. One gold was wrong and was corrected through the tool (`--accept-gold <id> --reason`, new: it also stores `gold_corrected` on the row): `proofing-diverse-dev::fv1_005791_0_0_lp2` resolved "he" to a name that appears only in the next question; a pronoun is not resolved by looking ahead (the message alone does not say who "he" is, the host resolves it). The gold rows that lack the `still`/`again` presupposition were re-baselined with the rest.
- **B. One result packet.** `abduce`, `diagnose`, `plan` and `simulate` (`reasoning/abduction.mjs`, `reasoning/worlds.mjs`) lower the typed facts, rules, hypotheses, actions and goal to knowledge wires (`reasoning/bridge/operations.mjs`) and ask the oracle's `abduce` and `plan` queries (simulate closes and reads the intervened world with the oracle); `associate`, `induce` and `analogize` (`reasoning/learning.mjs`) score supplied traces. All seven answer in the oracle's packet (`reasoning/bridge/packet.mjs`: `status`, `complete`, `guarantee`, `budget`, `used`, `notes`, `ignored`, `strategy`, and the operation's own field `hypotheses`/`explanations`, `plan`, `tests`/`next_test`, `whatif`, `candidates`/`patterns`/`mappings` with status `approximate`). Ported from the old packet to the native one: the plan's steps carry `binding`, `adds`, `removes` and `cost` (`js-reference/plan.mjs`); candidate hygiene (target removal, `rejected` with reason, generated candidates, actions not valid at the instant asked in `ignored`) is done before the question in the bridge. Removed: `reasoning/common.mjs` `packet`, `Budget` and `LIMITS` (the runtime's budget is the oracle's `Budget` with `OPERATION_LIMITS`), the fields `kind`, `epistemic`, `search`, `assurance`, `plans`, `nextTest`, `executedTests`, `factualStatus`, `interventions`, `sourceMemoryModified` and the statuses `candidates` and `patterns`; a counterfactual over non-causal rules is `not_expressible` (`reason causal_model_required`) and `unsupported` is left to rule 8. No controller was deleted: every one has a user (the SOP wires `abduce`, `diagnose`, `plan`, `simulate`, `associate`, `induce`, `analogize` are documented, executed by the wire-help tests and used by the skills and examples) and the oracle covers only the four logical ones, which now run on it. `deduce`, `temporal` and `classify` keep the host projection of the oracle's rows (`answers`, typed `proof`); DS006 "Result packet" states it. Regenerated: `sop/cnl.mjs`, `sop/outputs.mjs`, `sop/runtime.mjs` (output ports), `eval/signature.mjs`, `eval/contracts.mjs` (`guarantee` replaces `epistemic`), `examples/reasoning/scenarios.mjs`, the wire help pages `abduce`, `diagnose`, `plan`, `simulate`, `associate`, `induce`, `analogize`, DS006, `docs/wiki.html`; tests `reasoning-v3`, `grouped-consumers`, `contracts-boundary`, `eval-runner` updated to the native fields. No corpus gold used the old packet.
- **C. `wall` everywhere.** The wall-clock cut is `budget_exhausted` with `reason wall` in the oracle (`js-reference/budget.mjs`), `datalog-e10`, the tests and the docs; `time` is no longer a reason name (DS006, `docs/wiki.html`, the proposal).
- **Shared program reader.** `compileProgram` (`js-reference/program.mjs`) throws `NotExpressibleError` (feature `code_sandbox`) for a circuit that holds a `code` or `test` wire, so every strategy that uses the shared reader answers `not_expressible` and none crashes with `unknown_wire_type`; only `code-sandbox` runs those wires.
- **Glossary.** FormalizerLLM in `docs/wiki.html` and DS021 "Names, roles and datasets": distinct from SymbolicProofingLLM and from SymbolicLM, the model boundary applies to it, SymbolicLM is the chat default.
- Not fixed, reported: the shadow gate of `prolog-tabling` and the other strategies fails on smoke case 90 (`every_grouped`, supported by the oracle, not declared by the others); `sop/knowledge/validate.mjs` (linker-m1-agent, in progress) imports `lexicon-checks.mjs`, which does not exist yet, so everything that imports the knowledge validator fails until it is added.
