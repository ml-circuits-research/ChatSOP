pragmatic

A pragmatic wire is one advisory signal about the pragmatic or emotional role of a message or of a span of it: a greeting, thanks, urgency, a hedge, frustration, a possible irony. It is emitted by the host's EmotionDetectionSystem after the model, never authored by the small formalizer (the model language is unchanged: stated, assumed, unclear, query, constraint, unparsed). A signal is the system's subjective perception: useful symbolic input for the reasoner, not 100% verifiable, never a fact about the world, never evidence, never stored and never carried to a later turn. The normative contract is DS029.

The reasoner may use the signals only to regulate itself: urgency asks for a shorter answer and a faster strategy; frustration, anger or confusion re-check the previous interpretation or offer a clarification; a hedge makes the hedged statement a supposition (consistent with certainty hedged); a greeting or thanks without content gets a short courtesy reply and no computation; a possible irony forbids acting on the literal meaning without confirmation. The runtime reports the wire as {status: "pragmatic", advisory: true, kind, score, span, source, basis, near} and changes nothing else.

Every accepted keyword

KeywordCardinality / defaultAccepted value and concrete effect
kindExactly once; requiredOne kind of the closed set: greeting, closing, thanks, apology, politeness, urgency, frustration, anger, confusion, curiosity, joy, sadness, fear, disappointment, hedge, emphasis, profanity, offensive, irony_possible, confirmation_request (a tag question such as "right?"), topic_shift (a discourse marker such as "by the way") or unclassified (a pragmatic span that no strategy could name; the host does not emit it by default).
scoreExactly once; requiredThe strategy's confidence as a decimal from 0 to 1 with at most three digits (0.82, 1); pragmatic_score_form otherwise. It is the strategy's own estimate, not a calibrated probability; the host drops signals under minScore (default 0.5) and, per kind, the strategies whose measured precision is too low (config/emotion-detection.json).
spanAt most once; optionalOne nonempty JSON-quoted verbatim part of the message, at most 200 characters (pragmatic_span_form), that carries the signal. Without span the signal describes the whole message (a classifier reading the whole text). A leftover span of SymbolicLM that a strategy classifies appears here, and the matching unparsed wire is dropped; a span that stays unclassified remains unparsed.
nearAt most once; optionalnear $id: the wire the signal modifies (for example the hedged stated); one $id naming a wire of the same program (link_form; an unknown name fails the graph check). Without near the signal concerns the message as a whole.
sourceExactly once; requiredThe id of the strategy or model that produced the signal, lowercase letters, digits and underscore (symbolic, go_emotions, toxic_bert, llm).
basisExactly once; requiredHow the signal was found, from a closed set: lexicon (a word or phrase list), pattern (punctuation, case, emoji or a syntactic pattern such as a tag question), classifier (a neural classifier) or llm (a language model). Descriptive only; the reasoner never uses basis for trust.

Courtesy before a question

EN “Hi! Could you please tell me where Ana works? Thanks a lot!!!”

@p1 pragmatic
  kind greeting
  score 0.9
  span "Hi"
  source symbolic
  basis lexicon

@p2 pragmatic
  kind politeness
  score 0.85
  span "Could you please"
  source symbolic
  basis lexicon

@p3 pragmatic
  kind thanks
  score 0.95
  span "Thanks a lot"
  source symbolic
  basis lexicon

The greeting, the polite frame and the thanks are classified instead of staying unparsed; the question itself is formalized by SymbolicLM as usual. The three signals ask for no computation of their own.

A hedge attached to a statement

The hedge is consistent with certainty hedged on the statement: the reasoner treats the statement as a supposition, never as evidence.

A whole-message signal from a classifier

@p1 pragmatic
  kind frustration
  score 0.82
  source go_emotions
  basis classifier

Without span the signal describes the whole message ("ugh, again?!"): the reasoner re-checks its previous interpretation.

An unknown kind is rejected

The kinds are a closed set; a free label such as annoyed is not a kind.

@p1 pragmatic
  kind annoyed
  score 0.8
  source symbolic
  basis lexicon

The model may not author it

The small model writes only stated, assumed, unclear, query, constraint and unparsed; the declarative compiler rejects a pragmatic wire in model output.

@p1 pragmatic
  kind thanks
  score 0.9
  source symbolic
  basis lexicon
