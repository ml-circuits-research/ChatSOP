policy

Declares host-chosen upper limits for bounded exploratory operations. A policy controls execution resources, not the user’s mathematical or logical problem; model-authored declarative input cannot choose it. It cannot grant new permissions or increase the active runtime limit; operations may only tighten their existing limits.

Status and author: Host-approved declaration; a model-origin program cannot install this definition. A model-origin program is explicitly denied this declaration.

Accepted fields

Single means at most once, repeatable means zero or more, and required means at least once at parse time. Other field names are rejected.

Execution and result

No field is required, so an empty policy is syntactically valid but has no limits to apply. Every present field must be a finite integer in the stated range; numeric $wire references are not resolved by policy lowering. An exploratory operation such as abduce, diagnose, associate, induce, analogize, plan, simulate or temporal accepts a policy field referencing ~approvedPolicy. For each supplied limit, runtime uses Math.min(activeHostLimit, policyLimit). reason and solve do not have a policy field, and this declaration is not a general global configuration setting.

Current SOP examples

Bound candidate exploration

@tight policy
  maxCandidates 12
  maxHypotheses 4
  timeoutMs 1000

@question query
  where parent ana bogdan
  mode exists

@search abduce
  query $question
  policy ~tight

A trusted program can request stricter limits for this abduction. Without supplied hypotheses or evidence the operation may find no explanation; the policy does not fabricate candidates.

Limit planning

@planning_budget policy
  maxNodes 128
  maxDepth 4
  maxPlans 2

@destination goal
  where at robot station

@route plan
  goal ~destination
  policy ~planning_budget

The host’s active limits remain the ceiling. Without admitted action definitions and facts, a plan is not promised.

Invalid or unsafe example

@bad policy
  timeoutMs 0

The parser accepts the field, but policy lowering rejects zero: allowed limits start at 1.
