# Research direction and publication boundary

**Status key:** **VISION** is a proposed interpretation or research hypothesis, not a measured outcome. **PLANNED** specifies a proposed future study or publication action and does not itself authorize execution. **DONE (evidence: path:lines)** describes only what the cited current artifact actually establishes. **SUPERSEDED** identifies a historical manuscript statement that cannot be used under the current session decisions; the replacement is marked VISION, PLANNED, or DONE, not silently treated as an observation. In particular, no neural training, fine-tuning, optimizer run, model accuracy, independent human review, submission, acceptance, or peer-reviewed status is claimed here. The two source documents, previously `article/direction/Articol.docx` and `article/direction/Publicare.docx`, are preserved at `probably_obsolete/article-direction/`. Their extracted line numbers below refer to the archived documents. They are historical direction, not executable authority or proof of results (`docs/specs/DS000-vision.md:22-26`; `TODO.md:181-202`).

## Research question and intended contribution

**VISION.** Can a narrow language formalizer, host-compiled inspectable SOP circuit, separately selected memory and reasoning strategy, and controlled-language answer form a persistent agent whose errors and costs can be attributed to the right component? A fluent answer may use wrong premises, a syntactically valid program may encode the wrong question, and retrieval similarity does not prove a claim. The proposed contribution is an operational and epistemic contract joining conditional interpretations, approved knowledge and procedures, solver values, provenance, temporal validity, and reported coverage—not a new universal solver, an invention of logic or hashing, or proof that a small model matches a large one. A common typed representation permits independent memory/solver substitutions only within their declared compatible profiles; an incomplete retrieval cannot justify global uniqueness, absence, or optimum. See `probably_obsolete/article-direction/Articol.docx:15-25,53-67,169-175,231-237,325-353` and the current authority in `docs/specs/DS000-vision.md:8-24`.

**VISION.** PAL, LINC and Logic-LM already separate language interpretation from execution or proof; this project must compare the *persistent* knowledge/procedure contract and its attribution of intermediate effects, not claim that NL-to-program-to-solver is new. Scallop's Datalog/provenance, LMQL's constrained model-programming, DSPy's textual module optimization, PICARD's constrained decoding and MLIR's typed compilation are related but not interchangeable. RAG supplies external retrieval and may complement SOP document selection: the proposed distinction is that approved retrieved material can become justified premises/procedures and is evaluated before CNL, rather than merely conditioned prose. Bloom/stable Bloom and holographic reduced representations are antecedents to associative storage; the cortical/Thousand Brains analogy motivates complementary perspectives but is **not** biological validation, HRR-equivalence, or lossless-compression evidence. Modular cognitive architectures also precede this work; this bibliography is selective, not a priority or exhaustive-survey claim (`probably_obsolete/article-direction/Articol.docx:27-51,369-405`).

**VISION.** The language component may be small while symbolic knowledge, approved procedures, and algorithms carry other costs. The suggested 300M–800M range and Q4 weight arithmetic are design scenarios, not a proven optimum, actual file/RAM size, CPU/phone throughput, Romanian reliability, or evidence that 270M equals 70B. A teacher-assisted library also has a real preparation, review, and teacher cost. A negative result—1B needed instead of 270M, SQLite best on structured data, or approximate hints not improving retrieval—still tests the contract (`probably_obsolete/article-direction/Articol.docx:249-274,325-353`).

**DONE (evidence: `docs/specs/DS000-vision.md:12-20`; `eval/README.md:5-13`).** The *documented current boundary*, not a neural result, is model-authored `premise`, `query`, `constraint` only; host-approved compilation chooses `resolve`, `pack`, `solve`, `cnl`, `remember`, and `clarify` where appropriate. A `premise` is attributed conditional context, never a sourced `fact` or implicit write. Formalizer evaluations and trusted full-circuit evaluations have separate `evaluation_track` values. The host generates clarification with `pendingSop`, `required`, and `next: answer_clarification` when identity/required scalar selection needs it; lack of evidence alone stays `unknown`. A `constraint.select` requests a checked scalar: ambiguous values require clarification even when the underlying claim is entailed. This status records the *specified contract*, not a measured model implementation or a solved research question (`TODO.md:5-11`).

**DONE (evidence: `docs/specs/DS004-sop.md:37-47`; `TODO.md:7-10`).** The current syntax uses whitespace-separated atoms such as `works_at maria acme`, rejects `works_at(maria, acme)`, and gives quotes no literal-protection power for variable-shaped atom terms: `"?x"` is still classified as a variable after decoding. These are documented language boundaries, not claims of a model's adherence.

**VISION.** In the proposed system, exact scan/SQLite, Recall Weaver projections, Holo/H7 associative codes, and exact-evidence-plus-hint hybrid do not provide equal retrieval coverage automatically. Direct completion from strong finite-domain cues is not unconstrained enumeration. Hash membership checks verify that a retrieved tuple was stored, not that its source told the truth; domains, fingerprints, retention metadata and indexes belong in total cost. Reinforcement due to legitimate use is a retention policy, not independent epistemic confirmation; temporal correction/retraction, archive/pinned retention, fork, user isolation, generations and GC all affect longitudinal evaluation. JS finite/Horn reasoning, compatible SWI or Z3 execution, abduction, induction and planning have distinct interpretations: possible is not entailed; explanation is not observed cause; a simulated plan is not a real-world action. See `probably_obsolete/article-direction/Articol.docx:169-237`.

**DONE (evidence: `docs/specs/DS017-engine-comparison.md:8-43`; raw artifact `eval/reports/current/comparisons/engines.json:1-18`).** A bounded current *symbolic bank comparison* records query/corpus hashes, actual routes and retrieval outcomes for its stated sizes, seeds and bank configurations. Its memory bytes are not equal total budgets; warm bank timings exclude end-to-end model/formalizer cost; the documented hybrid hint trial showed no top-answer improvement on that small corpus. Z3 is unsupported for the Horn cells, not a third observed Horn engine. These observations are neither P9 longitudinal source studies nor neural comparisons and do not generalize to all workloads.

## Controlled experiments to preregister, not reported results

**PLANNED.** P9 has five access-controlled study conditions: **S0 source-only** uses the permitted source content without a reused implicit SOP library; **S1 dynamic repair** can propose, validate and apply a repair within the current task but does not carry it over; **S2 accumulating SOP** admits separately approved rules/procedures across eligible successive tasks; **S3 frozen library** uses a versioned snapshot with no new promotion during evaluation; **S4 teacher reference** measures what a qualified teacher produces under a declared access/cost regime, not a small-model oracle. Freeze the exact source access, external tools, proposal/promotion rules, temporal state, and candidate/accepted snapshots for each arm before comparing. Do not let a candidate rule, a quoted document or a model output silently become global authority; require counterexamples, independent source transfer and review before publication (`TODO.md:94-105,181-189`; `docs/specs/DS000-vision.md:18-24`; `probably_obsolete/article-direction/Articol.docx:61-67,274`).

**PLANNED.** Compare focused, bounded broad, adaptive and near-exhaustive expansion with measured call/time/token/memory budgets, stopping conditions, source order and cost to saturation. Measure per-family semantic accuracy and abstention, UNKNOWN calibration, semantic-gap recovery, cross-source transfer, approved-rule reuse, novelty and saturation; distinguish source gaps, formalization errors, missing semantics, solver failures and over-inference. Reuse must be tested on different questions and independent sources rather than only the trigger that motivated a rule. Include longitudinal corrections, rarely used but important knowledge, fork/restart and forgetting. Report coverage and unsupported/partial routes beside accuracy (`TODO.md:94-105,109-118,181-191`; `probably_obsolete/article-direction/Articol.docx:337-353`).

**PLANNED.** Separate the *mechanism* study (same trusted circuit, knowledge and admitted profile across memory/backend choices), *formalization* study (new EN/RO requests into only model-authored declarations), and *complete agent* study (source-to-answer effects). Formalizer ablations vary Romanian anchors (zero / hundreds / thousands), resolver on/off, approved-procedure availability on/off, and CNL alone versus optional verbalizer, one principal factor at a time. Memory comparisons include exact SQLite, Weaver, Holo/H7 and verified hybrid hints at measured total budgets; backend comparisons require the same supported semantics and actual native route rather than fallback. A syntax pass, symbolic gold execution, repository smoke, or historical 306-test count is not conversational accuracy (`TODO.md:167-191`; `eval/README.md:5-13`; `probably_obsolete/article-direction/Articol.docx:280-323,347-353`).

**PLANNED.** Baselines: small model in prose, qualified small model formalizing SOP, large model in prose, and large model with **the same approved knowledge and tools**; include teacher development cost and inference/runtime costs. Comparisons must not claim a small agent beats an unassisted large model merely because one side alone received a library. A separately authorized model study would require held-out Romanian questions and a localized source→formalization→execution→CNL answer trace. No such model run is authorized by this plan (`TODO.md:54,140-151,187-189`; `probably_obsolete/article-direction/Articol.docx:329-331`; `probably_obsolete/article-direction/Publicare.docx:29-31`).

**PLANNED.** Before any authorized run, freeze a directional hypothesis, one changed factor, comparator, falsification condition, source/case-group splits, provenance/rights, model and backend identities, hashes, library snapshot, seed policy, budgets, safety stops, metrics and denominators. Keep translations, paraphrases, shared source worlds and hard negatives grouped; seal the holdout from generation and checkpoint selection. Report paired uncertainty at independent case/source level—not pseudo-replication of paraphrases—across seeds and sources; expose exclusions, missing/invalid outputs, abstentions, confidence intervals where estimable, negative findings, incomparabilities and raw-result-derived tables. The promotion thresholds in DS011 are proposed gates to freeze, **not achieved scores**, and no dataset is training-qualified merely because a symbolic suite runs (`docs/specs/DS011-experiment-preregistration.md:6-34`; `TODO.md:109-118,181-191`).

## Publication route and evidence obligations

**PLANNED.** An English architecture-and-mechanism paper should maintain a claim→experiment→artifact→limitation ledger, distinguish archived from fresh measurements and explain its differences from PAL, LINC, Logic-LM, Scallop, LMQL, DSPy and RAG. The initial article may present reproducible **bounded** symbolic mechanisms with explicit missing neural evidence, never the superiority of a 270M agent. A separate, independently reviewed source-to-answer model study could support a later extended claim, but is not presumed successful. The archived Romanian v0.1 remains a draft requiring author review, not a published manuscript (`TODO.md:194-202`; `probably_obsolete/article-direction/Articol.docx:5-25,361-365`; `probably_obsolete/article-direction/Publicare.docx:5-11,25-35`).

**PLANNED.** Candidate outlets—not rankings, acceptance probabilities, submission commitments or currently validated policies—are Neurosymbolic Artificial Intelligence (architecture with an executable artifact; ask editors about conceptual-paper fit), NeSy (IR/linker/outputs/interpreters with controlled comparisons), Semantic Web (if formal representation, provenance, query interoperability and demonstrated tools/system maturity fit), JOSS (a later distinct software paper with actual public-development history and tests), and arXiv plus a versioned artifact (preprint dissemination, **not peer review**). The editorial annex describes NeSy 2026 as past and a possible 2027 edition, and describes initial PDF, open-science and AI-assistance requirements as of its own date; verify *current* venue scope, official calls, dates, format, disclosure, prior-publication, open-development and dual-submission rules at the official links **immediately before** any submission. No simultaneous duplicate journal submissions (`probably_obsolete/article-direction/Publicare.docx:11-23,25-51`; `TODO.md:193-202`).

**PLANNED.** Release only a stable, immutable version of code and compatible declared licenses, exact configurations/run identities, manifests and hashes, source rights and attribution, sealed-test rules, redistributable data **or lawful acquisition/reproduction instructions**, raw predictions and results, table-generation scripts, explicit backend/fallback/exclusion records and verifiable rerun instructions. A private conversation, unpinned external dependencies or working archive is not a public reproducible artifact; note the archived manuscript explicitly disclaims a public software DOI/release. Record actual author names and affiliations, funding and conflicts from the authors; disclose generative assistance and have authors actually review claims before any human-review declaration (`probably_obsolete/article-direction/Articol.docx:361-365,407-417`; `probably_obsolete/article-direction/Publicare.docx:25-35`; `dependencies.md:3,30-34`; `TODO.md:194-202`).

**DONE (evidence: `docs/specs/DS018-source-rights.md:21-39`; `eval/README.md:20-24`).** Rights findings are recorded, **not blanket permissions**: SQuAD v2.0 is CC BY-SA 4.0 with attribution/modification/ShareAlike duties in the sealed test-only reference; PAWS permits free use with requested acknowledgement subject to underlying text rights; QA2D **data** and ProofWriter are unverified, QQP is restricted, AmbigQA/AmbigNQ is unverified, and all these unverified/restricted assets remain quarantined. The QA2D code's MIT license is not a data license. Romanian translations inherit source duties. Training remains forbidden without a new explicit user approval bound to dataset, recipe, model and run regardless of data-rights status (`docs/specs/DS011-experiment-preregistration.md:34`; `TODO.md:54`).
