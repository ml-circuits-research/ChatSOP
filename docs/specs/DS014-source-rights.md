# DS014 — Source rights and redistribution evidence

This specification records what has actually been verified about the rights of each external corpus candidate, so that a dataset claim is never derived from a software license. A public repository, a mirror, or an "open" label is not evidence of a data grant. Every row below states the evidence inspected and the resulting status. Where only secondary evidence exists, the status of the *source text* is `unverified`, and that text stays in the local source cache `datasets_sources/`. Under the owner's decision of 2026-09-28 (next section), corpora that are *inspired by* a source, and copy none of its text, are released regardless of the source text's status.

## Owner decision of 2026-09-28: inspired-by corpora are released

The repository owner decided on **2026-09-28** that the derived corpora inspired by **QQP, PAWS, ProofWriter, AmbigNQ, QA2D and SQuAD** are released. This decision supersedes, for those derived corpora only, every earlier restriction on them; the dated acquisition sections that preceded it are archived in `probably_obsolete/specs/sections/DS018-dated-sections.md`. The reasons are recorded as the owner stated them:

1. **Original work.** The corpus texts are written or composed by this project. They are inspired by these datasets, not copied from them.
2. **Different purpose.** The sources serve paraphrase detection, reading comprehension, rule reasoning, ambiguity resolution and NLI; the ChatSOP corpora teach a formalizer to map a message to declarative SOP.
3. **What is taken is structure.** ChatSOP takes structure, label types, phenomena and statistics (question types, paraphrase operations, ambiguity types, proof depths, negation rates, rewrite operations). It never takes text: no source sentence, question, passage, answer, declarative rewrite, name, title or row identifier enters `datasets/**`, `eval/suites/**` or a published artifact, verbatim, translated or lightly edited.

Consequences:

- The released status is `inspired-by-released` (vocabulary below). It applies to the derived corpus, not to the source text: the cached source rows in `datasets_sources/**` keep their recorded status and stay a local research cache.
- The no-copy guarantee is **verifiable**. `node tools/datasets/no-copy.mjs` streams every cached source text and fails a corpus on any word 4-gram shared with the specific source row named in a row's lineage, on any word 8-gram shared with any cached source text, and on any case id, source row id or generator counter inside natural language. Its report is `eval/reports/current/rights/no-copy-<corpus>.json`. The diversity generator ([DS022](specsLoader.html?spec=DS022-diversity-generator.md)) runs the same check before it writes a corpus.
- Row provenance records `source.license` as the licence of the authored text (`MIT (repository LICENSE); original ChatSOP authored text`) and a `rights` block with `inspired_by[]` (source, URL, licence as known), `rights_decision: owner-released-inspired-by`, `rights_decision_date: 2026-09-28` and `text_copied: false`. `tools/datasets/rights.mjs` is the single source of these values; `null` licences are not written by current builders.
- Attribution is kept as a courtesy in [`datasets/SOURCES.md`](../../datasets/SOURCES.md), which lists each source, its URL, its licence as known, what was taken and what was not.
- The existing SQuAD v2.0 sealed source-reference derivative is unchanged and keeps its CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution, modification and ShareAlike duties.
- Training remains unauthorized until a new explicit approval (AGENTS.md rule 3). This decision concerns corpus rights only.

This is the owner's decision, recorded as an engineering fact; it is not legal advice (rule 5 below).

Applies to: any tool that imports or converts source text, `datasets/knowledge/source/**`, any evaluation or training corpus, and any published artifact. The model-language corpora (`formalizer-v1`, `formalizer-ood-v1`, DS022) are generated by this project and inspired by the released sources under the decision above: they carry no source text, every row records its inspiration sources and `text_copied: false`, and `tools/datasets/no-copy.mjs` proves it.

## Status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `cleared` | A primary license notice was read; attribution/modification duties are recorded and satisfied by the artifacts that use it. |
| `permissive-attribution` | The primary notice grants free use and asks for acknowledgement; no ShareAlike obligation was found in that notice. Upstream third-party content still needs its own notice. |
| `unverified` | No authoritative license notice could be located; secondary summaries disagree. Redistribution and gold export are prohibited until evidence is recorded. |
| `restricted` | The publisher's terms limit use (for example to research/non-commercial); not usable for training or redistribution without written permission. |
| `statistics-only` | Applies to a non-commercial, restricted or unlicensed source (owner decision Q-DATA-7 of 2026-09-28): it may be read only to compute aggregate statistics (rates, distributions, category proportions) that are recorded with the source name. No text, example, template, name or row of it enters a corpus, a generator table, a test, a document or a published artifact, verbatim, translated or paraphrased, and no generator family may be designed from its rows. |
| `inspired-by-released` | Applies to a derived corpus, never to source text: the corpus copies no source text, takes only structure, label types, phenomena and statistics, passes the no-copy check, and was released by the owner's decision of 2026-09-28. |
| `llm-authored` | Applies to messages an LLM wrote for this project under its provider's terms (owner decision D2 of 2026-09-29, section "LLM-authored text"): original, not copied, verified by the no-copy check and the sealed-suite guard, stamped with its provenance. |

## LLM-authored text (owner decision D2 of 2026-09-29)

The owner decided on **2026-09-29** (D2) that training messages may be written by an LLM, systematically and with verification ("OK. Can you do this with a Haiku agent? It's fine to diversify as much as we need. Can it be done systematically?"). The pipeline is [DS022](specsLoader.html?spec=DS022-diversity-generator.md) "LLM diversification".

| Asset | Version / source | Evidence inspected | Status | Consequences in this repository |

| Messages written by Claude Haiku 4.5 (`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`) through Claude Code headless (`tools/datasets/llm-diversify/`) | Generated per run; every call cached with the sha256 of its prompts | Anthropic's commercial terms assign the customer's rights in outputs to the customer and the Usage Policy governs use; no third-party text is supplied to the model except the project's own generated messages | `llm-authored` (original text, provider terms) | The message is original text written for this project, not copied: it must pass `tools/datasets/no-copy.mjs` against the cached sources **and** the sealed-suite guard (`tools/datasets/llm-diversify/sealed-guard.mjs`) against formalizer-v1 test, OOD and wild. Its target is inherited from the generator, never written by the LLM (paraphrase layer); distilled targets need two exact-agreeing formalizations plus the executable checks. Every row records `generation_trace.method`, the model id, the prompt hashes and the judge verdict, `source.authored_by` and `rights.authored_by`, and `quality_flags.llm_authored: true`, so an ablation can drop them. LLM-authored rows are at most 40% of a build. Prompts never contain a sealed-suite row, a wild writer persona or an annotation example. |

`llm-authored` joins the status vocabulary above for text that a model wrote for this project under its provider's terms; it is never applied to source text. Whether a later release of LLM-authored rows needs extra notices is re-verified against the provider terms in force at that time (rule 6).

## Rules enforced here

1. Source text with status `unverified` or `restricted` MUST NOT appear in any corpus export, gold target, or published artifact. Only `cleared` and `permissive-attribution` assets may be ingested as text, with the recorded attribution and modification notices. A corpus that is only *inspired by* such a source is `inspired-by-released` under the owner's decision of 2026-09-28, provided it passes `tools/datasets/no-copy.mjs`.
2. A software licence never implies a data licence, and a dataset licence never implies rights in the underlying third-party text.
3. The Romanian corpus follows the same rule: synthetic generation, or a source whose rights are recorded here first. Translation into Romanian creates a derivative and therefore inherits the source duties.
4. Training remains unauthorized regardless of this table; a rights-cleared corpus is necessary but not sufficient for the training gate.
5. A `statistics-only` source (Q-DATA-7) contributes numbers only: each use records the source, the statistic and the command that computed it next to the number (for example the typo-operation weights of the noise model), and the source keeps its own status row here before it is read. Candidate sources named by the data-expansion proposal (`eval/reports/current/data-expansion-proposal.md` section 3: GitHub Typo Corpus, DailyDialog, TimeDial, CLUTRR, MS MARCO, Yahoo Answers, ATIS, LaRoSeDa, MOROCO, RoNLI and the unlicensed ones) are `statistics-only` at most and need a row here before use.
6. This specification is an engineering record of verified notices, not legal advice. Where a row is load-bearing for a release, obtain written confirmation from the publisher first.
7. Every cached source folder under `datasets_sources/` keeps a `provenance.json` with the pinned URL, the retrieval date (an explicit unknown rather than an invented date), byte size, SHA-256, the conversion or extraction method and any failed licence lookup. `tools/datasets/analyze-sources.mjs` verifies those hashes before analysing, and JSONL converted from a source is itself source material under the same status. The AmbigNQ cache records primary evidence that the publisher's release archive bundles a CC BY-SA 3.0 licence notice; the row above stays `unverified` until the owner reviews that evidence against the underlying Natural Questions and Wikipedia obligations.
