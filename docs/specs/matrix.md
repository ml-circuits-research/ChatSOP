# Specification Matrix

The specifications DS000 to DS023 are numbered without gaps and listed here in file order. They were renumbered on 2026-10-01 when the tiny-model research branch was frozen; its specifications (training, data and evaluation, query curriculum, independent and research corpora, corpus audit, capability APIs) are archived in `probably_obsolete/tinyLLMExperiments/specs/`; the symbolic EmotionDetectionSystem was restored as DS023 on 2026-10-02. `aliases.json` maps every former id to its current spec, the spec it was merged into, or its archive location. Former ids remain only in frozen files (archives, the project journal, the append-only topic notes and historical reports), which keep the numbering of their date; `node tools/check-spec-refs.mjs` enforces this.
