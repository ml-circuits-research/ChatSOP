## Introduction

SOP (properly SOP Lang) stands for Standard Operating Procedure: SOP Lang is an attempt to formalize standard operating procedures, and the knowledge, questions and constraints they depend on, as typed, inspectable, executable text.

ChatSOP helps an operator or user turn natural-language requests into declarative typed [SOP](wiki.html#definition-sop) problems, statements and assumptions, expand them through host-approved execution steps against reviewed, time-aware knowledge, and receive an explicit result, evidence, and controlled-language explanation. Its symbolic knowledge and reasoning remain separate from language-model weights.

## Core Content

### Purpose and authority

The [formalizer](wiki.html#definition-formalizer) proposes only `stated`, `assumed`, `unclear`, `query`, or `constraint` declarations from a user message ([DS021](specsLoader.html?spec=DS021-model-surface.md)); the host validates them, selects the user and permissions, and compiles the inspectable execution circuit. When identity is missing or ambiguous, or a required scalar cannot be uniquely obtained, symbolic host orchestration can generate `clarify` and suspend dependent work rather than require the model to invent a question. A model `stated` or `assumed` proposition is a string report the host links and uses turn-locally, not a sourced assertion or a repository write; explicit trusted `fact` and `remember` operations remain distinct. The model and a supplied document must not grant their own permissions, install rules or policies, or certify a proof. A [verbalizer](wiki.html#definition-verbalizer) may restate an existing [CNL](wiki.html#definition-cnl) result but must not alter its truth status, provenance, quantities, time, or completeness. There is one executable SOP language with one model surface and no versions; other SOP dialects do not become compatible by sharing a name.

### Distinct information roles

Original documents and traces remain identifiable sources; accepted facts and approved definitions are the evidence used by the runtime; patterns and hypotheses are candidate objects, not observations. Source-backed publication requires host review and provenance. Adding approved knowledge need not retrain a model, and retraining does not itself publish a fact or prove a rule. The [memory engine](wiki.html#definition-memory-engine) and [reasoning strategy](wiki.html#definition-reasoning-strategy) are independently selected axes subject to their capability and completeness limits.

**Languages.** The reasoner and its knowledge are English-only. The small formalizer accepts a message in any supported input language (English and Romanian today; further languages are planned and will be added by extending the corpora, the fine-tuning and the host dictionary, not by changing this principle), including mixed-language and misspelled text. It does not translate (owner decision D1 of 2026-09-29): it recognizes intent and structure and normalizes the language, keeping content words in the message's language as lemmas; proper names stay as written. A deterministic host step translates the content strings with a reviewed bilingual and synonym dictionary before linking, reporting any unknown word as `untranslated` instead of guessing. The answer to the user is rendered in English by default or in the requested language by the host. Adding a language therefore changes the formalizer's training data, the dictionary and the host's answer rendering, never the knowledge or the reasoner ([DS021](specsLoader.html?spec=DS021-model-surface.md)).

**Current plan (owner decisions of 2026-09-30).** The current formalizer is SymbolicLM (Stanza grammatical analysis plus the UD-to-SOP rules), evaluated on clean English; the small formalizer LLM is earlier work whose results are in the archive and history reports. The chat's textToCleanEnglish step (user-validated, calling LanguageProofingLLM) turns bad input into acceptable English, and SymbolicProofingLLM rewrites correct English into the limited English SymbolicLM understands, only for what rules cannot fix. Names, the three datasets (`bad_english`, `symbolic_english`, `neuro_english`) and the policy (no fine-tuning until the owner approves after reviewing the datasets in the audit page; base models at 30 to 40 tokens per second on a laptop CPU) are in DS021 "Names, roles and datasets".

Two learning flows stay distinct: the narrow message-to-SOP model task, and a source-material-to-knowledge loop that extracts, generates questions, solves, diagnoses, repairs, validates, accumulates, and reruns ([DS011](specsLoader.html?spec=DS011-material-sources.md) for ingestion). The archived vision documents under `probably_obsolete/vision/` and their audit register under `probably_obsolete/specs/` are historical evidence only. Quoted source claims, reusable implicit-rule proposals, temporary problem state, and reasoner capabilities must remain separate. A candidate implicit rule is not made global because it repairs one question; executable HARD rules require scoped review, counterexamples, runtime probes and independent authorization. Neither flow establishes a self-modifying production server or automatically accurate small language model.

### Evidence boundaries

An untrained or absent model cannot substantiate conversational quality. A symbolic demonstration or historical report cannot establish neural accuracy, production security, hardware readiness, or comparative superiority. External corpus rights and model weights are not supplied by the source-code license. Publication claims require fixed inputs, observed runs, documented omissions and fallback routes, and independently reviewed evaluation; see [DS008](specsLoader.html?spec=DS008-data-evaluation.md). Work remaining is tracked in `TODO.md`, not asserted as delivered product behavior.

The research direction and publication materials archived under `probably_obsolete/article-direction/` are source goals, not peer-reviewed results; the research question they motivate is stated below, and the experiments and publication obligations are preregistered in [DS010](specsLoader.html?spec=DS010-experiment-preregistration.md). Data rights, reproducible run artifacts, independent semantic review, model identity, and backend execution must support every published comparison. Controlled generators, including Luna or another qualified small model, may draft paraphrases only after a representative pilot and cannot make final semantic or architectural judgments; an unexposed generator identity remains unknown.

### Research question and intended contribution

These statements are research direction (vision), not measured outcomes; no neural training, model accuracy, independent human review, submission or peer-reviewed status is claimed.

Can a narrow, context-free language formalizer, a host-compiled inspectable SOP circuit, separately selected memory and reasoning strategies, and a controlled-language answer form a persistent agent whose errors and costs can be attributed to the right component? A fluent answer may use wrong facts, a syntactically valid program may encode the wrong question, and retrieval similarity does not prove a claim. The proposed contribution is an operational and epistemic contract joining turn-local message statements and assumptions, approved knowledge and procedures, solver values, provenance, temporal validity, and reported coverage — not a new universal solver, an invention of logic or hashing, or proof that a small model matches a large one. A common typed representation permits independent memory and solver substitutions only within their declared compatible capabilities; an incomplete retrieval cannot justify global uniqueness, absence, or optimum.

PAL, LINC and Logic-LM already separate language interpretation from execution or proof; the project must compare the *persistent* knowledge and procedure contract and its attribution of intermediate effects, not claim that NL-to-program-to-solver is new. Scallop's Datalog and provenance, LMQL's constrained model programming, DSPy's textual module optimization, PICARD's constrained decoding and MLIR's typed compilation are related but not interchangeable. RAG supplies external retrieval and may complement document selection; the proposed distinction is that approved retrieved material can become justified facts and procedures and is evaluated before CNL, rather than merely conditioning prose. Bloom filters and holographic reduced representations are antecedents to associative storage; the cortical analogy motivates complementary perspectives but is not biological validation, HRR equivalence, or lossless-compression evidence. This bibliography is selective, not a priority or exhaustive-survey claim.

The language component may be small while symbolic knowledge, approved procedures, and algorithms carry other costs. Model-size ranges and quantized-weight arithmetic are design scenarios, not a proven optimum, measured RAM, device throughput, Romanian reliability, or evidence that a small model equals a large one. A teacher-assisted library also has a real preparation, review, and teacher cost. A negative result — a larger model needed, SQLite best on structured data, or approximate hints not improving retrieval — still tests the contract.

The documented current boundary, not a neural result, is that the model authors only `stated`, `assumed`, `unclear`, `query` and `constraint` from the user's message alone, using quoted strings and the closed role set; the host links those strings to knowledge and compiles `resolve`, `pack`, `solve`, `cnl`, `remember` and `clarify` where appropriate ([DS021](specsLoader.html?spec=DS021-model-surface.md)). Exact scan/SQLite, RecallMemory projections ([DS023](specsLoader.html?spec=DS023-recall-memory.md)), HoloMemory associative codes ([DS024](specsLoader.html?spec=DS024-holo-memory.md)) and exact-plus-hint hybrid storage do not provide equal retrieval coverage automatically; possible is not entailed, an explanation is not an observed cause, and a simulated plan is not a real-world action. Reinforcement due to legitimate use is a retention policy, not independent epistemic confirmation.
