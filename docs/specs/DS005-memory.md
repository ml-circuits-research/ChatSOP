## Introduction

The [repository](wiki.html#definition-repository) stores admitted claims and approved definitions so a user can ask about a time, correct knowledge, and maintain a private session without exposing it to other users or forks. The [memory engine](wiki.html#definition-memory-engine) retrieves candidate premises; it does not decide that a claim is true.

## Core Content

### Temporal identity and isolation

A claim's validity interval and the time at which the repository learned it must remain separate. Retractions and corrections retain provenance; a new job or changed location must not silently overwrite a previous claim or imply physical presence. Users, sessions, base forks, and referenced historical snapshots have distinct roots. `commit` publishes authorized session changes; `discard` removes uncommitted changes; an old referenced snapshot remains a valid [GC](wiki.html#definition-gc) root. `gc` without `--apply` reports unreachable candidates; deleting one still referenced is invalid.

### Physical providers

The `memory.engine` selection may choose `weaver`, `holo`, `sqlite`, `scan`, or `hybrid` using a common SOP-facing interface. SQLite and scan retain exact tuples; Weaver reconstructs from relational associative counters; Holo reconstructs argument candidates from signed distributed counters; hybrid uses an exact SQL bank with associative hints. Their representations and costs differ and cannot be assumed interchangeable on disk. The physical engine and reasoning strategy remain orthogonal. A retrieval candidate or associative score is not a proof, probability, independent fact, or permission to execute recovered code. Exhaustive enumeration, counting, and absence claims require an appropriate complete exact retained view; a successful bounded lookup alone does not certify that all historical facts survived retention.

### Retention and reporting

Sharded profiles maintain generation-specific banks, pinned knowledge, and referenced snapshot data with budgets and explicit maintenance actions. Promotion must preserve the original valid interval and provenance; strengthening an actually used nonhypothetical premise does not confirm every retrieved hint. Roots from other users and sessions protect reachable shards during GC. Comparison reports must account for metadata, retrieval limits, retention and completeness, and must keep unavailable backends or historical reports distinct from observed new runs.

### Legacy H7 and RecallSOP reference audit (2026-09)

**Implemented — physical H7 boundary.** The H7 kernel distributes each known key's value code across independently selected signed-counter banks, regenerates key masks and codewords from hashes, saturates writes, and ranks an *explicit* candidate domain by signal/correlation and margin. A sufficiently weak or close signal abstains; scores are not truth probabilities. A repeat detected by recovering the same value reinforces without novelty ageing; a novel value may age selected counters one step toward zero. `ageStepsPerNovel: 0` disables that particular ageing mechanism, as in the nonsharded Holo runtime profile. This is neither a universal fixed-space archive nor a guarantee of graceful application-level truth under erasure: the kernel needs candidate values and has no integrity receipt of its own (`memory/banks/holo-kernel.mjs:10-24,30-85`; `config/runtime-holo.json:34-43`; `tools/bench-h7.mjs:18-22`).

**Implemented — fact and wire adapters with different discovery limits.** The Holo fact bank reconstructs argument candidates from predicate/arity dictionaries and counters, and only returns sufficiently scored atoms that also have a matching SHA-256 receipt; those dictionaries and receipts grow outside the fixed counter arrays. Thus `boundedKernel: true` does **not** mean bounded total storage, a returned candidate is not exhaustive original-memory enumeration, and a receipt is not by itself evidence that the trace still survives. Separately, the wire adapter reconstructs a canonical SOP wire from an already known content handle, byte by byte, verifies its hash and parses it; it does not find unknown handles from partial semantic cues. These are distinct from the old proposal's two-plane, fully bounded cue-to-handle/content architecture (`memory/banks/holo.mjs:1-5,21-35,36-83`; `memory/banks/holo-wire.mjs:1-4,15-35`; `probably_obsolete/references/H7.docx:62-70`). Metadata and bank bytes must both be reported.

**Implemented — retention and generations, with profile-specific guarantees.** Legacy nonsharded `adaptive` retention performs occupancy-triggered cooling of the *normal* bank and may prune metadata when configured; pinned memory is separate and is not automatically cooled. `none` performs no automatic pressure maintenance. In the sharded layout, hot generations rotate on occupancy, claim count, metadata budget or configured age; immutable cold generations retain their own banks, never have counter arrays ORed together, and only predicate/arity routing skips shards without that domain. `bounded` can evict old *normal* cold shards by count, byte or configured age; `archive` does not evict automatically and fails explicitly at a configured archive-shard ceiling. Pinned capacity likewise fails explicitly instead of quietly evicting. A verified observed premise actually used in a proof may be promoted into a hot generation while keeping its original validity, earliest known time and provenance; speculative hints and conditional premises do not qualify. Sharded maintenance is rotation/eviction, not legacy blanket cell decay (`memory/temporal.mjs:6-15,37-81`; `memory/sharded.mjs:1-4,57-65,85-110,118-149`; `config/runtime-holo-sharded.json:9-42`).

**Planned / not implemented — proposal scope.** Do not describe text-trace mining inside the memory bank, two-plane partial-cue discovery without a growing index, utility-ranked SQLite eviction, or a fully bounded Holo wire store as available features. SQLite already provides exact tuples and optional lexical search, while hybrid currently writes facts to exact SQLite and an associative bank and returns exact verified facts with separate associative hints; it is not a universal store for unformalized experiences (`memory/banks/sqlite.mjs:11-18,74-76`; `memory/banks/hybrid.mjs:9-25`; `probably_obsolete/references/RecallSOP-proposal.docx:29-45,70-83,104-123`; `probably_obsolete/references/H7.docx:62-70`). The historical H7 synthetic 64 KiB/3,000-association and erasure figures are claims in the old document, not reproduced results of the present benchmark: the current benchmark explicitly calls its measurements new, uses separate known-handle wire tests, and labels forced top-1 accuracy separately from accepted-answer precision (`probably_obsolete/references/H7.docx:151-164`; `tools/bench-h7.mjs:1-22`; `tools/bench-memory.mjs:25-31,46-59`).
