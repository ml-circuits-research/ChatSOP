# Accepted fields in sop-agent-3

Generated by `node tools/capabilities.mjs --write` from the parser, declarative boundary and reasoning registry. “Unique” means at most one occurrence; required fields are listed separately. Semantic validation applies in addition to these field constraints. Only premise, query and constraint may be authored by the small model. Host-approved metaprograms compile them into inspectable execution circuits without inventing an identity or promoting a hypothesis to a fact.
