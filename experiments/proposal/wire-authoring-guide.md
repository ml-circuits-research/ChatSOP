# Wire authoring guide (moved)

The authoring guide of the reasoning-wires proposal now lives in the skill `skills/sop-wire-authoring/`:

- `skills/sop-wire-authoring/SKILL.md`: the task procedure for a subagent or an omp agent that compiles long texts into knowledge wires (inputs and outputs, the mandatory write-validate-fix loop, source and quote rules, closedness with a source sentence, reification, symbols and strings, no `jsEval`, the differential check, how to split long texts);
- `skills/sop-wire-authoring/authoring-guide.md`: the pattern guide with executed examples (the content of this file, adapted to the final documentation).

The language manual is the section "Knowledge wires" of `docs/specs/DS004-sop.md`, with one help page per wire under `docs/wire_typs/`.
