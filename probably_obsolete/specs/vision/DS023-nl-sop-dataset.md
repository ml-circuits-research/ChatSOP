## Dataset proposal: case unit, model boundary and sources

The unit is **semantic intent** (question, attached context, logical relations, ambiguity status), not a sentence or translation. Each case has a chosen canonical **declarative** SOP target; several English surface forms and selected Romanian anchors express that case. A linked lexical near-neighbor is a *different* case if argument order, polarity, scope, bindings, time, quantity, condition direction, or intended outcome differs. Split linked cases and semantic duplicates together, *before* paraphrasing and translation. A parser-only alpha key accommodates local renaming/spacing, but structurally different programs with finite-world agreement need separate review, not automatic equality. The host—not the small model—compiles the approved execution circuit. Source: D:5,8-19,55,98-121,123-134; current `docs/specs/DS008-data-evaluation.md:18,36,40`, `docs/specs/DS009-query-curriculum.md:20-25,38-44`, `tools/datasets/schema.mjs:108-119,123-179`.

D:14-19 calls the model's target a “canonical circuit / structure”; **superseded as model output**: small-model targets contain only `stated`, `assumed`, `unclear`, `query`, `constraint` (DS041) (`tools/datasets/schema.mjs:108-119`; `docs/specs/DS007-training.md:8,14`), whereas trusted host circuits own `resolve`, `pack`, `solve`, `cnl`, `remember`, `clarify` (`docs/specs/DS009-query-curriculum.md:38-61`). The current parser accepts whitespace-separated `works_at maria acme`, not parenthesized atoms (`docs/training.html:3`; `docs/specs/DS010-common-contracts.md:14`). Attached statements become model `stated` propositions (turn-local evidence or conditional suppositions), never sourced `fact` or implicit writes; authorized recording is a separately trusted `remember`, never an SOP `assert` alias (`docs/specs/DS009-query-curriculum.md:38,48,57-61`; `tools/datasets/build-curriculum.mjs:110-125,180-188`). Explicit `constraint.select` requests a checked scalar; if nonunique, clarification is required even when the general claim is entailed (`docs/specs/DS009-query-curriculum.md:44,50-52`; `tools/datasets/build-curriculum.mjs:122-125`). `clarify` is host-generated for missing/ambiguous identity or required scalar, carries `pendingSop`/`required`/`next` per the current-session decision, and is **not** a model target; absence of evidence alone is `unknown` (`docs/specs/DS009-query-curriculum.md:38,44,59`; `docs/specs/DS010-common-contracts.md:31,37`). `evaluation_track` separates historical trusted-system targets and declarative formalization; historical system rows are **not** evidence that the small model is being trained on them (`tools/datasets/schema.mjs:72,108-119`; `tools/datasets/build-curriculum.mjs:269-282`). Quoted atom strings do not preserve variable-shaped text: `"?x"` is still variable-shaped according to the session decision; do not treat quotes as an escape for such strings.

Use recommended datasets as *semantic scaffolding*, never ready-made SOP gold (D:21-34,149). QA2D questions/answers may suggest underlying proposition–query pairs, and ProofWriter facts/rules/proof graphs could preserve depth and answer status; **both data assets remain unverified/quarantined** (`docs/specs/DS018-source-rights.md:25-27`; `docs/specs/DS009-query-curriculum.md:22,94`). QQP's human equivalence/nonequivalence labels suggest natural paraphrases and confusable pairs, not targets; its release is **restricted** (`docs/specs/DS018-source-rights.md:28`). PAWS/PAWS-X high-overlap word-order contrasts could challenge bag-of-words matching: PAWS primary notice permits free use with acknowledgement, subject to underlying text rights; it has **not** entered this corpus (`docs/specs/DS018-source-rights.md:29`). AmbigQA/AmbigNQ disambiguated rewrites suggest ambiguity probes but rights are **unverified/quarantined** (`docs/specs/DS018-source-rights.md:30`). Romanian paraphrase pairs may diversify phrasing, never define semantic gold; rights must first be recorded under the same source-specific rule (`docs/specs/DS018-source-rights.md:35-38`). D:182-187 lists the upstream locator links, not data-rights evidence. The sealed SQuAD v2.0 dev reference is a **separate, test-only** source under CC BY-SA 4.0, not a substitute for QA2D/ProofWriter gold (`docs/specs/DS018-source-rights.md:25`; `docs/specs/DS009-query-curriculum.md:92-94`).

### Ten-step data pipeline (proposal, not an executed external-corpus build)

1. Acquire/cache each source with exact identifier, location, revision/checksum and verified data rights; quarantine unverified assets (`docs/specs/DS018-source-rights.md:23-38`; `docs/specs/DS014-material-sources.md:8-12`).
2. Normalize assertions, questions, answers and rules without rewriting meaning; retain immutable original bytes and quotations (`docs/specs/DS014-material-sources.md:10-14`; `docs/specs/DS008-data-evaluation.md:14`).
3. Build semantic cases: QA2D proposition and query; ProofWriter facts/rules/proof/depth/status; QQP/PAWS equivalence/contrast links only, not already formal gold. The external-source stage is **not implemented** (`datasets/query-v1/manifest.json:19-25`; `docs/specs/DS018-source-rights.md:26-29`).
4. Choose one canonical declarative SOP target, deterministic when justified; otherwise treat teacher suggestions as unapproved proposals pending independent semantic checks (`tools/datasets/schema.mjs:108-119`; `docs/specs/DS009-query-curriculum.md:105-109`).
5. Produce multiple English forms varying only controlled syntax, question form, framing, assertion order, politeness and lexicon (`tools/datasets/build-curriculum.mjs:137-148`; `tools/datasets/curriculum/cases.mjs:107-149`).
6. Add selected Romanian anchors on the *same case and target* after English target validation; source rights apply to translations too (`tools/datasets/build-curriculum.mjs:137-145`; `docs/specs/DS018-source-rights.md:37`).
7. Build linked, meaningfully discriminating hard negatives while minimizing lexical change; negative and anchor remain in one split (`tools/datasets/schema.mjs:149-155`; `tools/datasets/build-curriculum.mjs:203-209`).
8. Preserve ambiguity in case metadata and the problem; host-generated clarification addresses missing/ambiguous identity or required scalar, never unsupported knowledge or an empty proof alone (`docs/specs/DS009-query-curriculum.md:21,38,59`; `docs/specs/DS010-common-contracts.md:31,37`).
9. Layer parser/type/reference/allowed-origin, normalization, relevant-world execution, duplicate and adversarial checks, followed by independent semantic review; a symbolic pass is not gold certification (`tools/datasets/schema.mjs:68-119,123-179`; `docs/specs/DS008-data-evaluation.md:18,40`).
10. Group case, linked contrast and translation IDs **before** splitting; export physically disjoint case-grouped train/dev/test projections and retain integrity hashes. Export is not training authorization (`tools/datasets/build-curriculum.mjs:203-219,269-289`; `docs/specs/DS009-query-curriculum.md:111`).

## Failure-driven common-sense acquisition: preserved research design

C:3-20 asks whether a symbolic reasoner fed explicit source facts still lacks temporal, spatial, causal, agentive, pragmatic or default *connecting facts or rules*, and whether narrowly useful reviewed micro-theories saturate as new books are processed. It distinguishes a **source gap** (fact present in book but lost in extraction: re-extract), **semantic gap** (admitted facts lack a general linking rule: propose and test one), and **reasoning gap** (facts/rules exist but the backend cannot execute the needed semantics: change/extend a reasoner), C:23-29. More questions do not establish more truths; select bounded, extensible *reasoning families* and probes, not every possible natural question (C:31-54). These are distinct from NL-to-SOP model examples: source ingestion/review is an authorized host workflow, and user question assumptions are temporary (`docs/specs/DS000-vision.md:16-20`; `docs/specs/DS008-data-evaluation.md:14`; `docs/specs/DS014-material-sources.md:8-14`).

Four layers must remain distinct (C:56-61): **Source Knowledge** (passage-anchored facts/events/definitions/relations/constraints, persistent per book/project), **Implicit Knowledge** (reviewed scope-limited general rules/default candidates reusable across sources), **Problem State** (temporary hypotheses and facts, no automatic memory), **Reasoner Capability** (backend/interpreter/procedure infrastructure). Existing `docs/specs/DS014-material-sources.md:10-14` implements *unapproved* source drafts and explicitly forbids silent publication; `docs/specs/DS009-query-curriculum.md:21,38,83-84` separates facts and unsupported engines. A correct formal proof establishes inference from admitted facts, not faithful extraction, scoped rule correctness or real-world/task truth (C:63-75; `docs/specs/DS008-data-evaluation.md:8,18,28`; `docs/specs/DS010-common-contracts.md:26-48`). ENTAILED, CONTRADICTED, UNKNOWN, and PLAUSIBLE describe distinct epistemic claims; a conflicting proof is not a forced true/false verdict, an abductive explanation is not a fact, and absent evidence remains UNKNOWN (C:77-86; `docs/specs/DS010-common-contracts.md:28-52`).

A candidate rule is independent of the triggering answer; generalize it, test positive and exception/boundary counterexamples, declare applicability/scope and HARD/DEFAULT/PLAUSIBLE strength, demand independent reuse, and only then approve via host review (C:88-97; `docs/specs/DS000-vision.md:20`; `docs/specs/DS010-common-contracts.md:50-52`). No general DEFAULT compiler is present. Uncompiled dialects and abduction/causal/default/agentive/counterfactual families must be reported unsupported or hypothesis-level, *never* silently turned into hard Horn facts or model-authored `clarify` (`tools/datasets/curriculum/cases.mjs:5-13`; `docs/specs/DS009-query-curriculum.md:23,84,95`; `docs/specs/DS010-common-contracts.md:50-52`).

C:99-164 proposes an eight-stage **per-book host/review research loop**, not an existing autonomous deployed agent: A extract explicit source circuits with passage provenance; B generate selected-family questions at varied difficulty/depth; C solve and retain proof/trace + epistemic status; D diagnose source/semantic/reasoning gaps, genuine ambiguity, over-inference; E repair extraction or propose a general rule; F test positives, negatives, other questions; G approve and accumulate reusable rules in a registry only after independent authorization; H rerun old/new problems to quantify benefit and regressions. Compare focused, broad bounded, adaptive and near-exhaustive budgets *empirically*; freeze the library and use unseen questions, books in-domain, adjacent domains and independently authored/validated references. Proposed arms S0 source-only; S1 temporary LLM repair without persistence; S2 reviewed accumulated SOP; S3 frozen library on unseen inputs; S4 strong teacher/expert reference **not automatic ground truth**. Measure family accuracy, UNKNOWN calibration, gap recovery, transfer gain, reuse, new rules/source, LLM calls/problem, regressions/over-inference and cost to saturation. A saturation claim needs rising unseen-source performance and fewer *new reviewed* rules per source, not reuse in the generating case. The proposed Question Curriculum Generator, Failure Classifier, Semantic Gap Resolver and Implicit SOP Registry are a **prototype proposal**, not four existing production modules (`docs/specs/DS011-experiment-preregistration.md:12-26`; `docs/specs/DS008-data-evaluation.md:26-30`; `docs/specs/DS000-vision.md:20-26`).
