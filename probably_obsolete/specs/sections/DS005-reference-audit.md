### Legacy H7 and RecallSOP reference audit (2026-09)

**Implemented — physical H7 boundary.** The H7 kernel distributes each known key's value code across independently selected signed-counter banks, regenerates key masks and codewords from hashes, saturates writes, and ranks an *explicit* candidate domain by signal/correlation and margin. A sufficiently weak or close signal abstains; scores are not truth probabilities. A repeat detected by recovering the same value reinforces without novelty ageing; a novel value may age selected counters one step toward zero. `ageStepsPerNovel: 0` disables that particular ageing mechanism, as in the nonsharded Holo runtime profile. This is neither a universal fixed-space archive nor a guarantee of graceful application-level truth under erasure: the kernel needs candidate values and has no integrity receipt of its own (`memory/banks/holo-kernel.mjs:10-24,30-85`; `config/runtime-holo.json:34-43`; `tools/bench-h7.mjs:18-22`).

**Implemented — fact and wire adapters with different discovery limits.** The Holo fact bank reconstructs argument candidates from predicate/arity dictionaries and counters, and only returns sufficiently scored atoms that also have a matching SHA-256 receipt; those dictionaries and receipts grow outside the fixed counter arrays. Thus `boundedKernel: true` does **not** mean bounded total storage, a returned candidate is not exhaustive original-memory enumeration, and a receipt is not by itself evidence that the trace still survives. Separately, the wire adapter reconstructs a canonical SOP wire from an already known content handle, byte by byte, verifies its hash and parses it; it does not find unknown handles from partial semantic cues. These are distinct from the old proposal's two-plane, fully bounded cue-to-handle/content architecture (`memory/banks/holo.mjs:1-5,21-35,36-83`; `memory/banks/holo-wire.mjs:1-4,15-35`; `probably_obsolete/references/H7.docx:62-70`). Metadata and bank bytes must both be reported.

**Implemented — retention and generations, with profile-specific guarantees.** Legacy nonsharded `adaptive` retention performs occupancy-triggered cooling of the *normal* bank and may prune metadata when configured; pinned memory is separate and is not automatically cooled. `none` performs no automatic pressure maintenance. In the sharded layout, hot generations rotate on occupancy, claim count, metadata budget or configured age; immutable cold generations retain their own banks, never have counter arrays ORed together, and only predicate/arity routing skips shards without that domain. `bounded` can evict old *normal* cold shards by count, byte or configured age; `archive` does not evict automatically and fails explicitly at a configured archive-shard ceiling. Pinned capacity likewise fails explicitly instead of quietly evicting. A verified observed fact actually used in a proof may be promoted into a hot generation while keeping its original validity, earliest known time and provenance; speculative hints and conditional assumptions do not qualify. Sharded maintenance is rotation/eviction, not legacy blanket cell decay (`memory/temporal.mjs:6-15,37-81`; `memory/sharded.mjs:1-4,57-65,85-110,118-149`; `config/runtime-holo-sharded.json:9-42`).

**Planned / not implemented — proposal scope.** Do not describe text-trace mining inside the memory bank, two-plane partial-cue discovery without a growing index, utility-ranked SQLite eviction, or a fully bounded Holo wire store as available features. SQLite already provides exact tuples and optional lexical search, while hybrid currently writes facts to exact SQLite and an associative bank and returns exact verified facts with separate associative hints; it is not a universal store for unformalized experiences (`memory/banks/sqlite.mjs:11-18,74-76`; `memory/banks/hybrid.mjs:9-25`; `probably_obsolete/references/RecallSOP-proposal.docx:29-45,70-83,104-123`; `probably_obsolete/references/H7.docx:62-70`). The historical H7 synthetic 64 KiB/3,000-association and erasure figures are claims in the old document, not reproduced results of the present benchmark: the current benchmark explicitly calls its measurements new, uses separate known-handle wire tests, and labels forced top-1 accuracy separately from accepted-answer precision (`probably_obsolete/references/H7.docx:151-164`; `tools/bench-h7.mjs:1-22`; `tools/bench-memory.mjs:25-31,46-59`).
