## Current normative extraction

### Data, provenance and evaluation

A semantic case **MUST** retain its source ID, kind, URI/revision, checksum, license, transformation/generation trace, quality flags and truthful review status (including pending or unreviewed); preserve original passages as host-side evidence, not invented `premise.source`/`premise.quote`. Group translations, paraphrases, semantic duplicates and linked negative contrasts before assigning train/dev/test; reserve structurally distinct holdouts. Executable validation **MUST** check syntax, type/origin, split/hash boundaries and relevant-world outcomes, but **MUST NOT** be called proof of independent semantic review, model accuracy, or universal equivalence. The older Romanian distinction between a sourced “fapt” and a proposed “ipoteză” matters here: a model `premise` is conditional interpreted context, not either documentary provenance or a session write. `DS008-data-evaluation.md:8,12-20,24,32-44`; `tools/check-data.mjs:5-12`.

The current model surface is **only** `premise`, `query`, `constraint`. Atoms use whitespace (`works_at maria acme`); parenthesized atoms are rejected, and a quoted atom term that decodes to variable-shaped text still is not a protected literal. A trusted, host-approved layer compiles `resolve`, `pack`, `solve`, `cnl`, optional explicitly authorized `remember` (no `assert` alias), and `clarify` with `pendingSop`/`required`/`next` for missing/ambiguous identity or a required missing/nonunique scalar. Absence of evidence alone returns `unknown`; `constraint.select` asks for a checked scalar and nonuniqueness requires clarification even when the underlying claim is entailed. These **current-session decisions override the historical full-circuit/model-authored curriculum**, not vice versa. `DS004-sop.md:16-22,37-47`; `DS009-query-curriculum.md:38-61`; `DS008-data-evaluation.md:24,36-38`; `eval/README.md:5-13`.

Every result **MUST** identify `evaluation_track`: `formalization` for model-authored declarations and `system` for trusted whole circuits. Preserve source, manifest/version, eligible/attempted/missing/invalid/abstained denominators, separate status, answers, proofs, side effects, completeness, and actual execution route. Query `supported`, `refuted`, `both`, `unknown` must not be collapsed; numeric `possible`, `entailed`, `inconsistent`, `optimal`, `unsupported` or incomplete outcomes retain their declared profile. A bounded or timed-out search does not become refutation, and an `advanced` route that fell back to JS is not native solver evidence. A finite-world gold execution confirms only that fixture's behavior; a real model prediction and separately reviewed meaning are necessary before any model claim. `DS008-data-evaluation.md:26-30,34-42`; `DS015-solver-qualification.md:23-46`; `DS017-engine-comparison.md:43-45`; `eval/README.md:5-17,50-56`.

Only external assets **recorded** as `cleared` or `permissive-attribution` in `DS018-source-rights.md` may be ingested, exported or published, with their recorded attribution, modification and third-party-content duties. QA2D *data*, ProofWriter, QQP and AmbigQA remain quarantined; a software license or an archive's checksum cannot clear their data rights. Source clearance and symbolic fixture validation **never** authorize training: no training, fine-tuning, optimizer step, neural inference or training smoke run is authorized by this document or this corpus. `DS018-source-rights.md:8-10,12-39`; `DS007-training.md:24-29`; `DS008-data-evaluation.md:40-42`.

### Retention, generations and coverage

Select the real physical provider (`weaver`, `holo`, `sqlite`, `scan`, `hybrid`) independently of the reasoning strategy and state its configuration. In nonsharded adaptive retention, pressure cooling targets normal counters and can prune their metadata; pinned is separate and not automatically cooled. In generational mode, hot shards rotate by configured occupancy, count, metadata or age; `bounded` evicts old normal cold shards, `archive` preserves them until an explicit capacity failure, and pinned capacity fails rather than silently deleting pinned records. Promotion **MUST** be restricted to metadata-verified observed premises actually used in a nonhypothetical proof and preserve their original validity/provenance. It does not validate a source or reinforce a mere hint. `memory/banks/factory.mjs:7-19`; `memory/temporal.mjs:37-75`; `memory/sharded.mjs:61-110,118-137`; `DS005-memory.md:16-30`; `config/runtime-adaptive.json:9-19`; `config/runtime-sharded.json:20-32`.

Shard routing is deterministic by predicate/arity; banks are reconstructed independently, not ORed into a virtual bank. Every result must distinguish `complete` **within the retained, budgeted view** from survival of all historical facts. Snapshot/base, user and still-open session roots (including forks and explicit pins) protect reachable immutable shard objects; garbage collection validates reachable checksums and is a dry run unless applied. Validity time and `knownAt` are distinct; retraction/event control must not be lost merely because an old normal bank was evicted. A bounded normal-bank allocation is not a bounded total memory/disk promise: pinned banks, source/claim metadata, dictionaries, exact SQL indexes, open session roots and correction journals count. `memory/temporal.mjs:83-114`; `memory/sharded.mjs:37-55,85-137`; `memory/repository.mjs:55-96,150-179`; `DS005-memory.md:12-30`.

Exact `scan`/`sqlite` can enumerate the **retained** records when their search finishes; neither makes a source true. Weaver and Holo reconstruct candidates from associative banks with growing domain/receipt metadata; receipt membership and a large signal are not a proof of complete recall. Holo's bounded signed-counter kernel is not a bounded whole application, and the known-handle Holo-wire experiment is not semantic cue-to-handle retrieval. `hybrid` supplies exact SQL evidence plus independent associative hints, never hint-only admitted facts. Do not promise unrestricted absence, global uniqueness or count from a probabilistic lookup or an incomplete exact one. `DS005-memory.md:16-32`; `memory/banks/holo.mjs:21-50,67-83`; `memory/banks/hybrid.mjs:9-25`; `tools/bench-h7.mjs:18-22`.

### Comparisons and release gates

Predeclare the *same* facts, SOP queries, answer oracle and time/as-of snapshot when changing a memory bank; hold the reasoner constant. For reasoning comparisons hold admitted premises and representation constant, identify native backend, selected route/fallback/skips and its supported domain. Declare study-specific probe, shard, time, memory, disk and (if ever authorized separately) compute budgets **before** measurement. Report completion, false tuples/omissions, precision/recall, exact-set success and cost of banks **plus** domains, receipts, metadata and indexes. Separate forward completion, absent queries and high-fan-out inverse enumeration; measure bank-only build/retrieval separately from repository, parser, solver, network, checkpoint and whole-dialog costs. A differential pass on a synthetic small suite is not an end-to-end neural result or a universal engine ranking. `tools/bench-memory.mjs:2-4,16-31,34-59,63-75`; `tools/bench-shards.mjs:2-5,16-53`; `DS008-data-evaluation.md:26-30`; `DS017-engine-comparison.md:21-45`; `DS015-solver-qualification.md:33-46`.

For a **hypothetical later explicitly authorized** GPU run only, perform preflight before substantive device or artifact work; require exclusive machine-wide GPU worker ownership, adequate host/device memory and disk, pinned model revision and immutable qualified dataset/recipe, a writable owned output and a verified checkpoint before resume. Do not implicitly download/install, overwrite unfamiliar checkpoints, kill unrelated workers or treat a CPU/gold-copy/dry-run as training evidence. The current gate requires a new user approval bound to the exact qualified run; until one exists **do not launch train, resume, optimizer, neural inference or smoke training**. The previously observed Podman preflight is infrastructure evidence only. `DS007-training.md:18-29,31-58`; `training/cli.mjs:39-59,109-161,164-195,197-243`.

An offline release check **MUST** fail visibly on incomplete/stale verification partitions and keep any optional-backend `skipped`/`unsupported` cell separate from an observed pass. The active command is `node tools/verify.mjs`, whose current groups and fingerprinted `--collect` behavior are in `tools/verify.mjs:25-84`; it executes dry-run reports, not training, and proves neither neural quality nor source rights by itself. Public artifacts must still pass the separate `DS018-source-rights.md:23-39` gate. Do not recycle an old `verify.js`, `SHA256SUMS.txt`, benchmark figure or historical result as an observed current release outcome. `tools/verify.mjs:1-16,25-84`; `eval/README.md:11-13,27-29`; `DS007-training.md:24-29`.
