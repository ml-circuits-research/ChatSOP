# Generate reviewed NL-to-SOP examples

Before designing targets or data, read `docs/wire_types.html` and check syntax against the executable grammar. `@id type` declares a wire, `$id` consumes a value, `?x` is a local logical variable, and `~id` references an approved definition. Atoms use `[not ]predicate term1 term2 ...` with one to four whitespace-separated terms and no parentheses or commas. JSON double-quoted terms preserve spaces and punctuation. The small model authors only `premise`, `query`, and `constraint`; keep that declarative intent distinct from the host-expanded execution circuit. It does not choose session recording, retrieval, identity-resolution plumbing, or result rendering.

**Quoted text in generated targets:** Follow [DS004](../../docs/specs/DS004-sop.md) and the [shared quoted-text syntax](../../docs/wire_typs/syntax.html#quoted-text). Preserve natural proper-name spelling and capitalization; spelling alone does not establish a canonical entity ID. Encode free-text fields with JSON-style double quotes, including one-word names. In atoms, `"multi word, punctuation"` is one string term; escape embedded `"` and `\` using a JSON encoder, not single or smart quotes. JSONL encodes the entire SOP text as an outer layer. Text and quote fields keep quoted sigils inert, but a variable-shaped atom string such as `"?x"` decodes to a logical variable and is rejected as a ground fact/premise. Do not claim quoting alone makes that atom term literal.

The immediate priority is to validate that the **small formalizer model** reliably produces declarative SOP from natural language; evaluate this task separately from ChatSOP as a complete system and from the coding-agent workflow for documents and knowledge. `jsEval` does not belong in small-model targets, examples, or training, and computations hidden in `value.data` are not a substitute. Arithmetic/comparisons in supported `constraint` fields are declarative. A necessary identity or scalar clarification is generated by the host when genuinely unresolved, not authored as a model `clarify` wire. Unknown evidence alone is not a clarification request. `jsEval` remains a restricted expression interpreter, not full JavaScript; future full-JavaScript support is unspecified.

**Problem formulation is not code generation:** Teach declarative AND, inclusive OR, explicit nested grouping, comparisons, and numeric constraints as the model's task. Executable `all`/`any`/`end` blocks apply to `query.where/filter` and `constraint.require/claim`; use these blocks for Boolean combinations rather than parenthesized Boolean formulas. Atom arguments use whitespace, while arithmetic leaves retain genuine expression punctuation. Preserve the condition tree and correlated assignments in independent semantic oracles; do not flatten alternatives into conjunctions. Selected/filter variables must be guaranteed across alternatives or by a shared conjunct. Host-approved metaprograms may build inspectable `resolve`, `pack`, `remember`, `solve`, and `cnl` circuit steps without inventing identities or promoting hypotheses to facts.

Existing mixed corpora can contain `jsEval` and other targets outside the small-model boundary: mark them pending migration and independent review, not training-approved merely because they parse. This does not prohibit host-authorized, controlled computation by reviewed coding-agent programs; such execution plumbing and algorithms are distinct from declarative model targets.

Read `docs/legacy/requirements/07-date.md` before building new executable SOP worlds and targets. Use the prompts in `server/llm.mjs`, not a parallel version. Every example has a profile, group, and split. Do not move paraphrases between splits.

Generate linguistic variations from a fixed target. Check time, negation, question scope, and identity; parsing alone does not approve a target. Track genuine host clarification scenarios separately from formalizer targets, including unanswerable questions where appropriate.

Keep user assertions supplied as question context separate from persistent knowledge extracted from documents and approved with a source. A contextual assertion does not automatically become a published fact or ground truth; mark unknown premises and verify every quote, provenance, negation, and time interval before approving a target.

After the target/profile migration and independent review, run `tools/check-data.mjs --execute` for new or revised data. Maintain a human holdout and distinct logical compositions. Document the teacher model, parameters, sources, and review decisions. Do not call programmatic examples “validated natural Romanian” without review; do not train without separate user authorization.

For the v3 profile, separate from the formalizer's minimal curriculum, also read `docs/legacy/requirements/27-date-si-invatare-v3.md`. The type catalog is `sop/contracts/wires.json`; the existence of types and examples involving abduction/planning does not make them small-model targets or require reducing them to factual questions.
