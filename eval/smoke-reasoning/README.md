# eval/smoke-reasoning

Smoke suite for the proposed reasoning wires (`experiments/proposal/reasoning-wires-proposal.md`, round 3). It is a proposal artifact: nothing under `sop/`, `reasoning/`, `lib/` or `datasets/` was changed to make it run.

- `cases/<id>/` holds `knowledge.sop` (the knowledge circuits), `query.sop` (the query circuit), `expected.json` (the strategy-neutral expected answer, the `requires` feature list and the validator `warnings` the case declares) and a short `README.md` naming the reasoning feature. A case with `memory.json` runs behind the retrieval layer (cases 20 to 23).
- `validator.mjs` is the standalone validator of the proposed grammar: arity 0 to 6, named roles on `predicate` (`args subject:entity object:entity`, `args none`), type checks of constants, `closed` checks (errors: `absent_needs_closed`; warnings: `aggregate_needs_closed`, `count_needs_closed`, `every_needs_closed_scope`), time semantics (`at`, `during` = throughout, `overlaps`, `start_of`, `end_of`), the method step grammar (`choose`, `any_order`, `if/else`, `until ... max N`, `pick`, `optional`, `achieve`), the wires `norm`, `procedure`, `amendment`, `argument`, `trace`, governance fields, `overrides` checks, a reserved `x_` prefix for generated wires, and the slice-aware check `absent_over_incomplete_rules`. `invalid/` holds circuits that must be rejected, each with the expected code(s) on its first line. `node eval/smoke-reasoning/validator.mjs --grammar` prints the grammar table.
- `run.mjs` validates everything (the cases, the invalid fixtures and the DESUGARED form of every case), runs the self-tests of the comparison and of governance, then runs one adapter per strategy (`adapters/`) on every case and reports pass, fail, not expressible, planned or unavailable. It also prints the DECLARED coverage of the planned strategies (`exp` or `n/e` per case). A strategy declares the features it supports; a case that needs another feature is "not expressible", never silently weakened.
- `lib/desugar.mjs` rewrites `default` (strict contrary, `overrides` by fire, exceptions) and `integrity` into core rules over the wires in force; `lib/governance.mjs` selects the wires in force (approval, version, `asof`, supposed proposed wires); `lib/conditional.mjs` is the universal two-run lowering that gives `conditional` as a list of assumption ids (and `nonmonotone`); `lib/closed.mjs` is the host rule for counts and `every` over open predicates; `lib/compare.mjs` compares a normalized result with `expected.json`; `lib/memory.mjs` and `lib/widen.mjs` model the retrieval layer.

Run: `node eval/smoke-reasoning/run.mjs [--adapter id,id] [--case text] [--verbose] [--markdown]`. The private SWI-Prolog and Z3 binaries under `tools/.solvers/` are used when no `SWIPL_BIN`/`Z3_BIN` is set (nothing is installed). The zip engines are read from `datasets_sources/experiments_unpacked/` (gitignored) and are `unavailable` when it is absent. The report goes to `eval/reports/current/smoke-reasoning/report.json`.

Cases 30a to 37b (modes of work: methods with choice, norms, versions, amendments, conformance) have no live strategy yet: they are the acceptance tests of the planned `htn-strips-planner`; their expected answers were derived by hand and are to be confirmed by the shadow run of that strategy. The cases that engines express (09d, 09e, 10c, 10d, 35c, 35d, 07d, 08d, 04b, 23) pass on E10 and soplab, which checks the desugaring, the governance filter and the host rules.

Round 3 (review round 2 of the proposal): `lib/conditional.mjs` is now PER ROW and VERIFIED (a verification run on observed facts plus the leave-one-out set; if it fails the row is conditional on all assumptions with `conditional_unknown`; cases `10c` to `10f`); `lib/used.mjs` makes `used` one sufficient support set (the host's deletion method verified by a replay, `used_incomplete` otherwise; the harness replays a strategy's `used` alone in the oracle `js-reference` and compares statuses and rows, never leaf sets; cases `13c`, `13d`); `lib/compare.mjs` checks `used_support`, `used_incomplete`, `row_conditional`, `relaxed`, `obligations_triggered` and `compliance.deviations`; `lib/memory.mjs` and `lib/widen.mjs` retrieve a `default` like a rule and treat its head and exception predicates as completeness-sensitive (case `24`; hard distractors may be negative with `neg: true`). New cases: `12g` (count under during), `37c` (conform with `at`), `37d` (method deviation), `38` (obligation scoping), `39a` (advisory hard norm relaxed), `39b` (norm conflict of equal strength). New validator warnings: `obligation_unscoped`, `binding_on_soft_norm`; `action` carries the governance fields; `procedure` has no `version`; an amendment's `approval` is `proposed|approved|rejected`; a trace step may end with `at DATE`. The planned strategy `sopr-lift-worlds` is renamed `worlds-sopr`.

`adapters/js-oracle.mjs` runs the NEW strategy `reasoning/strategies/js-reference/` (the oracle of the proposal: a complete, deliberately naive implementation of the core, beside the `reference` and `advanced` routes). It passes every case it declares (modes of work are declared `not_expressible`); `run.mjs` replays `used` in it. The strategy folder holds copies of this directory's parser, governance and desugarer so that product code does not import from `eval/`; after the owner's review `sop/` becomes the single grammar source and the copies go.

Round 3 fixes (review round 3): `validator.mjs --authoring` is the mode of a wire author (the host-written governance fields `approval approved_by approved_at` are ignored with the warning `governance_ignored`, and `approval_incomplete` is left to ingestion); an absent `binding` is `strict` (the check `strict_forbid_conflicts_method`); the norm flag `standing` marks a standing obligation (warning `obligation_unscoped`; `standing` on a non-`oblige` norm is `standing_needs_oblige`), and a variable of an `oblige` pattern that no `when` atom binds is now the error `unsafe_variable` (the fixture `invalid/obligation-unscoped.sop` became `obligation-unbound-variable.sop`; `obligation-standing.sop` and `standing-on-forbid.sop` are new); `run.mjs` has an `authoring self-test`. `expected.json` of case `30a` lists the three actions in `used`. Backward compatible for every case.

Datalog strategies (datalog-agent, cases 65 to 69): `datalog-souffle`, `datalog-e10` and `datalog-soplab` are product strategies under `reasoning/strategies/` (the two zip engines are vendored there), registered as the adapters `datalog-souffle.mjs`, `datalog-e10.mjs` and `datalog-soplab.mjs`; the old read-only adapters on the unpacked zips stay as `zip-e10.mjs` and `zip-soplab.mjs`, labelled legacy (ids `zip-datalog-*`) and not registered. Cases 65 to 69 hold the smoke-size instance of five shapes (ring components, three-layer negation chain, grouped aggregates, a selective bound query, dense nonlinear recursion); `bench/datalog-scale.mjs` builds the same circuits at 10^3 to 10^6 facts with the answer known by construction, and `node eval/smoke-reasoning/bench/datalog.mjs` writes the speed table to `eval/reports/current/smoke-reasoning/datalog-speed.json`.

Planner and conform (planner-agent, cases 40 to 49): `conform-core` (`adapters/conform-core.mjs`, `reasoning/strategies/conform/`) is the oracle with the `conform` capability through the lowering of a trace and its norms and methods to core rules, and `htn-strips-planner` (`adapters/htn-strips-planner.mjs`, `reasoning/strategies/htn-strips-planner/`) is the planner of proposal 7 and 8; the stub of the planner is removed from `planned.mjs`. They pass 11a to 11c and 30a to 39b (the relational cases 35c and 35d stay with the oracle) and the new family: 40 (an amendment that removes a norm, one that tightens), 41 (a contested norm binds and is flagged, a rejected one never binds), 42 (`until` that exits, `until ... max` exhausted), 43 (an `on_failure` chain, `abort`, an advisory method that falls back), 44 (a standing obligation), 45 (`asof` and versions by step time in a conformance check), 46 (an advisory hard norm relaxed, not relaxed when a legal plan exists, and in a trace), 47 (the qualifiers `before`, `after`, `at_most_once`), 48 (blocked by an unmet requirement), 49 (a plan-length horizon, the step forms `pick`, `any_order`, `achieve` and sub-tasks, `prefer`). `lib/compare.mjs` checks `blocked`, `contested`, `obligation_unscoped`, `scope_unknown`, `choices` and `notes_include` when a case states them; `lib/conditional.mjs` now also counts the governed wire or amendment that a query supposes with `if` as an assumption (cases 30c, 36, 40a, 40b). Two expected files of the earlier family were corrected, with the reason in their READMEs: `30d` (the status of an abduction is `hypotheses`) and `32` (the valve predicates are now `closed true`, otherwise `requires not` has no evidence). The oracle's policy reader gained the default `binding strict`.
