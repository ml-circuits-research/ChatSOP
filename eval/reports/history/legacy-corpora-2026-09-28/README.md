# Observations of the deleted corpora (archived 2026-09-28)

These logs and reports were produced by builders, validators and reviews of the corpora that were deleted with the regeneration of the model-language corpora (DS022: `formalizer-v1` and `formalizer-ood-v1`): the query curriculum (v1, v2), the independent, pilot, research, grounded and source-reference suites, and the retired Markdown case tree. They were moved out of `eval/reports/current/` because they no longer describe any current file. They are historical observations, never current runs.
